AVAILABLE NMLS FIELDS FOR LICENSE ANALYSIS SECTION

CONTACT & WEB PRESENCE:
- Multiple Websites: Companies often have 2-3 different website URLs that can be displayed as clickable links
- Toll-Free Number: Separate toll-free phone number in addition to the main phone number
- Fax Number: Company fax number for business communications
- Street Address: Physical business location address
- Mailing Address: Mailing address which may differ from the street address

BUSINESS IDENTITY:
- Other Trade Names: Current alternative business names the company operates under (huge for prospecting)
- Prior Trade Names: Historical trade names the company previously used
- Prior Legal Names: Previous legal entity names before corporate changes

CORPORATE INFORMATION:
- Formed In: State or country where the company was legally formed
- Date Formed: Exact date when the company was established
- Fiscal Year End: Company's fiscal year end date (e.g., 12/31)
- Stock Symbol: Stock ticker symbol if publicly traded (most show "None")

REGULATORY & SCALE:
- Sponsored MLOs: Number of Mortgage Loan Originators sponsored by the company (indicates size)
- Regulatory Actions: Whether the company has any regulatory actions or violations on record
- Branch Locations: Number of active and inactive branch locations with link to view all
- Total Licenses: Complete count of all licenses including active and inactive ones
- Federal Regulator: If the company is federally regulated and by which agency

ENHANCED LICENSE DETAILS:
- License Numbers: Specific license numbers for each state license
- Original Issue Date: When each individual license was first issued
- Renewed Through: Expiration date for each license
- License Status: Detailed status (Active, Inactive, Revoked, Suspended, etc.)
- Authorized to Conduct Business: Whether each license authorizes active business operations

ADDITIONAL CONTACT METHODS:
- Multiple Email Addresses: Some companies have multiple contact emails listed
- Department-Specific Contacts: Different contact info for different business units
