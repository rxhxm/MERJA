Available Fields from NMLS Data:
Business Information:
Toll-Free Number
Fax Number
Mailing Address
Street Address - Physical location
Other Trade Names - Current alternative business names (like "1st Family Mortgage", "My City Mortgage", etc.)
Prior Other Trade Names - Historical trade names
Prior Legal Names - Previous legal company names
Formed In - State/country where company was formed (e.g., "Alabama, United States")
Date Formed - Exact formation date (e.g., "03/28/2000")
Stock Symbol - If publicly traded (most show "None")
Regulatory & Compliance:
Sponsored MLOs - Number of Mortgage Loan Originators sponsored
Regulatory Actions - Whether company has regulatory actions (Yes/No with link)
Federal Regulator - If federally regulated
Branch Locations - Number of active/inactive branches with link to view all
License Details (Enhanced):
License Numbers - Specific license numbers for each state
Original Issue Date - When each license was first issued
Renewed Through - License expiration dates
License Status - Active, Inactive, Revoked, etc.
